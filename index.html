How I work

My core method is systemic coaching with parts work, sometimes called the inner team. We all have different inner parts with their own voice and agenda. Once you can see them, you get to choose.

01

Map your inner team

We name the parts that drive how you act: the cautious one, the driven one, the one that wants to run away. All of them are welcome.

02

Notice who's in charge

You learn to spot which part is active in a given situation, in the moment rather than afterwards.

03

Practise a new response

From there, we practise choosing differently, in the real situations you bring to each session.

The work is present-day and practical. It's coaching, not therapy, and I keep that line clear. My practice is also informed by yoga and yogic philosophy, which I draw on where it helps.
